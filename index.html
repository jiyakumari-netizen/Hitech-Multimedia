About HiTech Multimedia

HiTech Multimedia is a professional photography and videography studio based in Balidih, Bokaro. We specialize in wedding coverage, cinematic videos, album designing and aerial drone cinematics. Our team uses industry-grade cameras, lenses and lighting to produce polished photos and videos. We also offer end-to-end album production including printing and framing.
